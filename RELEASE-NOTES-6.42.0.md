## Decisions taken

1. **`#/plan/<id>/household` keeps its address and draws its own screen** (`plan.household`): `household` stays a rail step (STEPS unchanged) and joins routes.js `PLAN_SUB_STEPS` so `screenName` picks `HouseholdScreen`; steps.js `SUB_STEPS` / `ALL_SUB_STEPS` are unchanged (it is not an editing sub-step of "Your plan"). The plan page's household section and "Does it last?"'s partner block link into it.
2. **The link is written on this plan only**, exactly as the planner's `setHouseholdPartnerId` does; V7 reads it either way (your-plan.md 6). Unlinking writes null. A plan in the other store cannot be chosen (only the plans the page holds are offered).
3. **Writes a locked plan still takes follow the planner's own lock rule** (LockedPlanGuard.js: the partner link, the budget and the dismissal are outside the lock): the link, "Keep things as they are", the shared budget and who pays go past the guard on a locked plan as the planner's saves do; the split never does — copies instead, the planner's rule. The lead may overturn this and refuse every write to a locked plan.
4. **"Use this split" writes the five keys `settingsWithSchedule` changes**, one write per plan, not the planner's whole `stressTool.settings` map; the plan the engines then read is identical (gate 9 holds `effectiveStressSettings` of both equal). The lift (`splitWrites`, `splitCopyNames`, `copyUnlockWrite`) keeps `applySplit`'s own writes byte-identical.
5. **The copies go through the one create**: the first without its link, the second linked to the first, then the first's link as one write through the one write function (`F.addDoc(` and `F.collection(` stay at one occurrence each; `updateDoc` is still called in one place). In a window the pair plus two copies never fit the three-plan limit, so the step refuses with "full" before anything is made and the button is not drawn.
6. **The survivor and care questions are asked in ages**, turned into the planner's "years from now" by the person's age today (whole years) in the run, so the figures are `runSurvivorCheck` / `runCareCheck`'s exactly; defaults are the planner's (10 and 15 years on, 70%, 50%, 3 years, £90,000) said as ages.
7. **Every figure is exact** (no "about £" on a plan page, the record scope's rule); the planner's `about()` rounding is not used in the preview.
8. **The planner's partner read is corrected** (`effectiveStressSettings` for the partner too); the gate's planner path uses the corrected read. Harmless on the fixtures (their two ages agree).
9. **The two-plan Undo** carries the partner's previous values on the toast (`others`); Undo writes the partner back first, then this plan, each past the guard.
10. **The named states are few and small**: seven household states; the working, failed, window, confirm, dismissed and single-question states are made in the render test from them by the reducer. The pre-existing plan states are left as pinned (a rebuild would only add the empty `household` key and float noise).
11. **The year-by-year tables are behind "Show the years"** rather than always drawn, so a phone sees the verdict first.
