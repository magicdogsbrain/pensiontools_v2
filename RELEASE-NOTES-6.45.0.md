## Decisions taken

Each is the builder's; the owner can overturn any of them.

1. **A new budget line or one-off cost is named first.** A nameless line is dropped by the planner's own save (`cleanBudgetForSave`), and in V7 every change is a save, so "Add a line" asks for the name beside the button and writes the line with it (the amount box is then focused). The planner lets a blank line sit on the page until the save.
2. **The budget is grouped by the planner's two tiers (Essentials, Lifestyle)**, not V7's own headings, so no new key is written. The planner's transient `_bandOpen` flag is not written; whether a line's ages are open is read from the line (an age other than the plan's ends) or kept in the draft.
3. **A shared budget's write carries the saver's `derived` onto the household's plan**, as `ScenarioRepository.saveActiveBudget` does (the gate says field for field); a change to who pays over time therefore writes the household plan when its totals differ.
4. **`decision.spStartDate` is written only where the Decision settings already carry the key** (even as null); a plan whose Decision tool never set it is left alone. The planner's Stress save writes only the Stress key; the Decision tool has its own box.
5. **`income.staircase-motion` is built, not retired.** The brief retired it; `tests/v7/parity/ledger.test.js` enforces the owner's rule of 2 Oct 2026 that no income-shape row may be retired. A CSS transition on the picture's bars, off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
6. **The years one by one and the pots' floors take the plan's own length and 2.5% a year prices** with no box; for a way of drawing other than Pots & Valves only its own table is shown.
7. **`inc.lump-sums` is marked built**: the cards (6.41.0) plus where each lump sum lands (6.45.0) are the planner's option on a saved plan; the question forms' "Money you expect to come in" (spending-shape.md §17) is a design for the questions, not a planner option, and is not a ledger row.
8. **"Am I on course?" stays a hand-over** to the planner from "What you pay in" (the ledger's `save.on-course` was not in this step's list).
9. **The exports' place is `{ tab: 'decision', sub: 'history' }`** (the planner's History page carries the per-year spreadsheet and the documents) with a button on the plan page's "Where you are" step, beside the plan document.
10. **A gilt line with no sub-class is given the index-linked profile** in `Holdings.proportions` (every ladder's rungs are index-linked); the planner gave no reason to leave it out.
11. **No named-state files were added** for the budget, saving or assumptions pages: their render tests draw them from the corpus through `stateWith`, and the named states (hold, move, way, …) exist for pages whose runs are expensive to make.
12. **The plan-* and d-retired browser specs' lists of the preview's own storage keys now include `pt_lastSeenVersion`** (What's new marks a first visit seen since 6.44.0; `plan.spec.js` already had it).
13. **Groups 1 and 2 (budget and saving) share one commit**: their wiring (schema, reducer, write effect, routes, screens) is one change; a split would not build at the first commit.
