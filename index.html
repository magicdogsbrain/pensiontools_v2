Starting Pot & Allocation

Holds least in shares when the pot is largest and most exposed to an early crash, then raises shares once that danger passes (Pfau-Kitces). Typically helps the worst cases but can slightly lower typical growth — run it and compare. Your risk level stays the average.

Break Glass HODL (Emergency Reserve)

Break Glass Reserve: Emergency fund only accessed when main strategy would otherwise fail.
