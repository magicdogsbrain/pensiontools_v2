# When the plan started: year 0 for everyone (design, 3 Nov 2026)

*Written from five readers' reports on the code (3 Nov 2026) and our rule that the model is for every situation, never bespoke. Release numbers: 6.48.0 is the small-fixes release already in flight (the sign-out, the preview's missed month, the notes, filling in missed months), so the work here starts at 6.49.0. The starting-situations research (reports under the session's scratchpad, to be folded into research/ when written) is the check-list this design is read against. Nothing in it is built until we have settled section 6.*

Found 3 Nov 2026: someone who stopped work in a past tax year and has drawn the plan's first step every month since is told by the tool that the plan "starts" in a later tax year, that the months since are a "run-up" paid "from SIPP cash", and that they are not compared with the plan. The plan's year 0 should be the tax year they stopped in; today is a later plan year; the pot entered today is the pot at this point of the plan; a ladder's cash years until its first rung are the strategy's own timing, not the plan's start. And the rule for the design: make anything like this generic. People may be retired but planning something to start in the future; people may not yet be retired; there are many situations, and none of them may be designed as the only one.

## 0. Where the mistake came from (why, before what)

The plan's "year 0" was never defined as the tax year retirement income began. On 9 Sep 2026 (6.4.0) it was defined as the tax year the *strategy's* rungs and tracks begin: the module header says so (`src/services/PlanTiming.js:9-10`, "Tax years before it are bridge years (money is being spent, but the plan's rungs and tracks have not started)"), and the release note of that day chose, for every existing retiree, "the same next-April start as before, so nothing moves" (`src/releases.js:1797-1812`; `migrateTiming`, `PlanTiming.js:161-162`, derives a retiree's start as `thisTY + 1` and never lets a derived year be in the past). Retirement itself has no date anywhere in the model: `retired` is a flag, `retireAge` is for the future only. The research trail records the welding of the two ideas in one breath — a retired test plan whose year 0 had been set two tax years after the year its person stopped work (the 6.4.0 entry in `research/qa-audit-7-sep-2026.md`) — while the earlier, right instinct is in `research/persona-test-2-aug-2026.md:7` ("for a retired person year 0 = now").

Everything before year 0 then needed a name, so "bridge" (6.4.0) became "run-up, paid from your SIPP cash" (6.11.1, `src/releases.js:1505-1512`) and was mechanised: the ladder's `bridgeCash` dial ("SIPP cash to the first April", `index.html:3123`; `src/strategies/GiltLadderPlan.js:99`), the `bridge` life stage (`src/services/LifeStage.js:33-37`, chosen at `:88`), the Decision tool's bridge alert and record stamp (`src/services/legacyDecision.js:654-657`, `:717-720`; `src/models/Decision.js:126-135`), plan-vs-actual's exclusion (`index.html:11971-11982`, the sentence reported at `:12006`), the document's run-up branches (`src/services/PlanDocument.js:494-512`, `:586`; `src/ui/components/PlanDocumentView.js:147`, `:357`), and the 6.13.5/6.15.0 rules that pin a derived year for good (`src/storage/StressRepository.js:109-119`) and treat any past start on a draft as stale (`PlanTiming.js:365-377`). V7 banned the words and kept the model (`src/v7/copy/banned.js:79-97`; `research/v7/rail-screens-language.md:74`).

Two facts decide the shape of the fix:

1. **The timing arithmetic already carries a past start.** Verified with node on 3 Nov 2026 (a made-up retiree, `firstTaxYear: 2023`, age 63): `deriveTiming` gives mode `retired`, "already running", steps from the age reached in 2023/24, the State Pension in its own plan year; `planYearOf(now, 2023)` is 3; `LifeStage` gives `running`. What is wrong is the entry points (no box can set a past year: `PlanTiming.js:217`, `:389-395`; `src/answers/plan/about.js:158-162`, `:233`; `src/services/PlanFromSeed.js:616`; `index.html:4792-4793`), the default (`:161`, `:239`), the staleness rule (verified: a 2023/24 draft is offered "Update it to start now (2026/27)?"), and the words.
2. **The engines have exactly one clock.** `planFromSettings` runs from plan year 0 with the pot entered as year 0's pot (`src/strategies/stressTest.js:158-173`; `potScaleOf` is 1 for a retiree, `src/services/PotsAtRetirement.js:26`; `simulationConfigFromSettings` the same, `src/services/SimulationConfig.js:48-59`), `buildGiltLadder` buys years `k = 1..N` from `firstTaxYear` out of today's pot (`GiltLadderPlan.js:102-107`) and `fullGiltTest` hands it the plan's own year 0 (`stressTest.js:621-626`), `deriveCompareConfigs` costs years 1..N discounted from now (`src/strategies/compareRunner.js:108`, `:172`), and the Decision plan of record is built from today's balances at year 0 (`src/services/PlanCommit.js:252`; `src/services/DrawdownService.js:56`). Forcing a past start in today makes the figures worse: the years already lived are bought again from a pot that already paid them, and the engines reader's run found the ladder unaffordable ("no index-linked gilt covers 2027": the linker that matured in 2026 is no longer in the list, `GiltLadderPlan.js:108-111`).

So the design is not a wording change and not a timing-module change alone. It is one new fact about the person, one new derived number, and one transformation that lets every engine stay year-0-based.

## 1. The model, in one paragraph

Four things, kept apart. **(1) When the person stopped work, or will** — a month, past or future (`stoppedWork`). **(2) When the plan's income started, or starts** — year 0, `firstTaxYear`, any tax year past or future; its default is the tax year of (1), and the person may change it ("I stopped in {year} but my plan's drawing starts {later year}; until then I live on X"). **(3) Today's position in the plan** — `planYearNow = planYearOf(now, firstTaxYear)`, signed: negative means before the start (still working, or stopped and waiting); 0 or more means in year N. The pot entered today is the pot *today*: projected forward to year 0 when the start is ahead (as now, `potScaleOf`), the pot at year N when the plan is running. **(4) A strategy's own timing relative to year 0** — the ladder's cash years and first rung, the bond tent's glide, the bridge's age — never the plan's start. The one derived number that makes this work is `elapsedYears = max(0, planYearNow)` (with `monthsIntoYear`): the engines are handed **the plan as seen from today** — the remaining steps, the ages, the State Pension year, the duration and the strategy's timing all shifted by `elapsedYears`, from today's pot — and their results carry `yearOffset: elapsedYears`, so the document, the where-am-I strip, Month by month and the household place them at plan year `elapsed + k`. The words on every page follow (3): "before the plan's income starts" only when `planYearNow < 0`; a plan that started in the past says so ("started October 2023; plan year 3 now"). When `elapsedYears` is 0 — every plan saved today — the transformation is the identity and every figure is byte-identical.

### I. Moving to another plan ("you have retired and lived off a plan, but now you want to change and transition to another plan")

The person stopped work, lived on plan "Ladder" with its months recorded, and from a chosen month moves to a new plan "Shares and cash".

- **The new plan's year 0 is the tax year of the change-over month.** Recommended over "keep counting from the stop date" because every engine, every ladder, the cones, the plan of record and the document count from year 0 with the pot *at the start* — a new plan from a new pot with a new schedule is a new plan, and year 0 should not do two jobs. The position in retirement is not lost: `stoppedWork` is the person's fact and is copied to every plan ("retired since October 2023; this plan from April 2029, its year 0"), and the new plan's `before` says what came before it.
- **The old plan is closed, not edited.** Today's lock rule stands: a locked plan is never changed. Closing writes two keys at the scenario root, outside both settings maps (so no checksum moves and the migration rules of `src/storage/migrations.js:13-19` are respected): `closedAt: 'YYYY-MM'` and `nextPlanId`. Its months stay readable; nothing after `closedAt` may be recorded on it.
- **The new plan is a new document** made from the old one's figures: `PlanCopy.copyOf(…, { carryHistory: false })` (`src/services/PlanCopy.js:50-61`: a draft, no document, no plan of record), with `stoppedWork` copied, `firstTaxYear` = the change-over tax year, `previousPlanId` and `before: { kind: 'plan', planId, from: 'YYYY-MM' }`, and its pots seeded from what is held today — the Transition tool's "Moving to your plan's mix" already turns a holdings ledger into a plan's mix (`src/services/TransitionPlanner.js:272-281` for the sequence; the ledger is `scenario.holdings`), so the close action reuses it rather than asking for the pots again.
- **Linked both ways**, like `household.partnerScenarioId` (`src/storage/ScenarioRepository.js:742-745`): `nextPlanId` on the old, `previousPlanId` on the new. The partner's link is re-pointed to the new plan on the person's say.
- **Where you are and the plan document** read the new plan from its start and point back: "Under your previous plan ‘Ladder’ until March 2029 — its months are there." Never "a bridge on cash".
- **What "closed" means for every tool that reads it:** Stress tester and Strategies — read-only, as locked, with a banner naming the successor; Month by month — no new months after `closedAt` (refused with the reason), the recorded months and tax years readable, plan-vs-actual frozen at the close; Where you are and the plan document — frozen at `closedAt` ("Closed March 2029; continued as ‘Shares and cash’"); Transition / What you hold — the ledger moves to the new plan (it is the person's, not the plan's); Accumulation planner — hidden; Household — a closed plan is not a partner candidate, its successor is; Budget — shared forward (`budgetTool.sharedFrom` already exists); the V7 rail — stage `closed`, one line and a link; the plan list — under "previous plans". `LifeStage` gets one key, `closed` (leads nothing, everything read-only), chosen when `scenario.closedAt` is set.

**As built (5 Nov 2026, the release after 6.54.0).** The rules are `services/PlanClose.js` (the offer, the new plan, the writes) and `services/PlanClosed.js` (what closed means, read by every tool and by the lock guard); the planner's line and question are `src/ui/planCloseUi.js`; V7's is the plan menu's box on "Your plans" through `planWrite.js` (kind `close`). What the design left open, settled: (i) `closedAt` is the LAST month the old plan covers, the month before the change-over month; `before.from` is the change-over month, the new plan's first. So "Closed March 2029" and "until March 2029" read as the design words them, and Month by month refuses a month after `closedAt` on the old plan and a month before `before.from` on the new one. (ii) The change-over month is any month after the last one recorded (a recorded month is never left past the close), up to twelve months ahead. (iii) Closing a plan that is not locked (unlocked after its records) locks it in the same write, so a closed plan reads as locked in every tool; the guard also treats any plan with `closedAt` as locked, with no unlock and no move of its start, and refuses its months after the close, the tax years after it, what you hold and its tick-offs. Its months up to the close stay writable (a missed month can be filled in). (iv) The planner offers the move in Stress tester → Settings, on a line under the lock's banner: that is where a locked plan says how it can be changed, and moving is the other way to change a plan someone has lived on. (v) What you hold travels with the copy; the old plan keeps its record as it stood, frozen. The budget travels with the copy too; a partner's plan that reads its budget from the old plan is re-pointed with the partner's link, on the person's say. (vi) `flexibleAccess` (J) has its place marked (`PlanClose.CARRIED_FACTS`) and nothing of it is built. (vii) The words say "carried on as ‘…’" where the design says "continued as ‘…’" — "Closed March 2029; carried on as ‘Shares and cash’" — to match the rest of the build's words ("the plan that carries it on"); both apps say the same. Not built, for us to decide: what happens to a closed plan when the plan that carries it on is deleted (today it stays closed, its line naming no successor, and its next-step banner opens its months instead).

**After review (5 Nov 2026).** (a) A closed plan's plan document, its past versions, its plan of record and its archive are frozen by the guard as well (the unlock archived them before its own write was refused), and nothing offers to unlock it or write a new document. (b) A change-over still to come (up to a year ahead) leaves the plan in its stage until its last month has passed: it is lived on until then, its banner and list line say "Closes at the end of …", and Month by month takes its months up to the close. (c) The new plan's pots are always asked, in boxes filled in from what is known: the holdings record for each kind of account it holds, the latest month recorded when that is newer, else the plan's own figures — each said where it came from. (d) The new plan reads its pots as today's once its first month has come, whether or not it says when work stopped, so the months of its year 0 before the change-over are not paid twice; closing an unlocked plan records where its pots stood, as every lock with no document does. (e) A copy is never linked: the four keys are not carried. (f) The partner is found either way, as V7's household reads it; the household of a closed plan is read-only, and a plan's own previous or next plan is never offered as its partner's. (g) V7's close makes the new plan the main plan when the closed one was.

### J. Back to work ("the same as above, but go back to work and accumulate more")

The drawing plan closes at the month work resumes (`closedAt`, `nextPlanId`); a **saving spell** follows on the new plan — `retired: false`, `stoppedWork` = the second stop (ahead), the Accumulation planner's pot record and pay-ins, exactly case A — and `before: { kind: 'working', from: 'YYYY-MM' }` with `previousPlanId`. Then the new drawing plan from the second stop is the same plan locked (A), whose "stopped work" date is the second stop.

The one thing that does not carry by itself is the **Money Purchase Annual Allowance**. Today the planner derives "triggered" from *this plan's* history (`index.html:9666-9669`: any record with a tax-free slice) and warns at £10,000 (`src/services/AccumulationEngine.js:103-107`; the £3,600 floor at `:83`). The new plan's history is empty, so the flag must be carried as the person's fact: `flexibleAccess: { since: 'YYYY-MM', fromPlanId }` at the scenario root, written when a drawing plan is closed (and copied by every later close), read by the planner beside the history. (Note for us: the rule is triggered by any flexi-access drawdown income, not only UFPLS slices; the carried flag should be set when a drawing plan is closed with any taxable draw recorded, which is a widening of today's test.)

**Is "a start, an end and a ‘before this plan’ reason on each plan, linked to the previous plan" enough?** Yes for the sequence itself: `stoppedWork` (the person's, copied), `firstTaxYear` (start), `closedAt` (end), `before` (reason: `working` | `plan` | the chosen later start of D), `previousPlanId`/`nextPlanId`. It is not enough on its own for two facts that belong to the person, not a plan — `flexibleAccess` and the household partner link — which the close action must carry. No "timeline" object is needed: the sequence is read by following the links.

**Which the first releases carry:** I needs only the close keys, the copy, the Transition reuse and the words — it can follow the re-anchor release. J needs the MPAA carry and the planner reading it — later, and B (drawing while working) after that.

**As built (5 Nov 2026, the release after 6.57.0).** J is the close's second reason, not a second close: `PlanClose.successorOf(…, { reason: 'working', stop })` and `closeWrite(…, { reason })` (the same two keys and guard allowance; only the lock words of a plan closed while unlocked differ), offered beside "Move to a new plan" in both apps when the plan knows the person's age (`closeOfferOf().working`). What the design left open, settled: (i) the second stop is asked as a month or, when the month is not known, an age (`workingStopOf`: after the change-over month and this month, at most 85, and leaving at least five years before the plan's end age); a month is kept as `stoppedWork` with the age reached in it as `retireAge`, an age alone leaves `stoppedWork` blank — About's own two ways of saying a stop ahead. (ii) The saving plan's year 0 is the tax year of the second stop, and every figure counted from year 0 moves with it as for I (`successorSettings`), so its income steps keep their ages and it ends at the same age; both copies carry that year 0; `potAtRetirement` is projected as the planner's Save projects a saver's. (iii) The Accumulation planner's figures are started, not guessed: the age today, the age it stops at, the pension pot today, and this month's pot record once the spell has begun; what goes in is asked on the saver's own pages ("What you pay in", the check-in, the planner's Accumulation page), and the planner opens the new plan as a saver's (without an account, on its Accumulation page; signed in, on its stage's opening page). The plan's record of the answer it was kept from describes the spell before and is not carried. (iv) `before: { kind: 'working', from }` with `previousPlanId`; the closed plan says why it closed by reading that plan ("Closed March 2029: back to work; carried on as ‘…’.") — the close writes nothing more on it — and without the successor at hand it says only "Closed March 2029.". (v) `flexibleAccess` (`services/FlexibleAccess.js`) is written by every close, either reason, when the plan closed carries it or has any taxable draw recorded (income drawn, `sipp > 0`, or a slice taken partly tax-free, `taxFree > 0`): `since` the first such month, `fromPlanId` the plan it was recorded on; copied unchanged by later closes; never written on the closed plan, whose own copy the guard freezes. The stored fact is what the close saw, so the readers and the close also follow `previousPlanId` through the plans at hand (`flexibleAccessAlong`, `carriedFlexibleAccess(plan, plans)`) and take the earliest taxable draw along that chain: a month filled in on the closed plan after the close, a month recorded before a change-over set up ahead, and a plan a 6.56.0 move made (which carries nothing of its own) all count. Absent everywhere, the readers make today's test of the plan's own records. (vi) The readers: the planner's Accumulation page (`mpaaOf` into `contributionWarnings`: the rule said whatever is paid in, a warning above £10,000, with the month; once the £10,000 limit applies the £60,000 carry-forward line is not said, since carry-forward cannot raise it); the preview's "What you pay in" and check-in carry the rule on their allowance line (the same words, and what goes in a year when it is more). "Am I saving enough?" opened from the plan reads the person as already drawing (B's `you.alreadyDrawing`), so B's own answer says its line on the limit when what goes in is over it, in B's words; the plan's verdict on "What you pay in" draws no warnings of its own (the allowance line says it). A plan kept from that answer carries the fact on (the draft keeps it from the plan, `draft/load`), while the answer still says the person has drawn. (vii) Fixture `19-back-to-work` (the closed drawing plan) and `19-back-to-work-saving` (the saving plan carrying the fact). (viii) A change-over set up ahead: the saving plan exists at once but begins at `before.from`; until then its check-in and pot record say when it begins and take no month (`PlanClosed.notBegunOf`), and the store refuses a saver's pot record or check-in dated before it (`successorRefusal`), as it refuses Month by month's. Not built, for us to decide: asking the fact when a plan is made (§8) is still to come; a copy carries the stored fact only (a copy is linked to no plan, so it has no chain to follow).

## 3. The data

### Added

- **`stressTool.settings.stoppedWork: 'YYYY-MM' | null`** — the month work stopped or stops. Written by the Timing block (a month box beside "I have already retired" / "I will retire at age"), the setup wizard, V7's About step (`stopMonth` accepting a past month instead of refusing it, `src/answers/plan/about.js:226-233`; `copy/about.js:16`), and `PlanFromSeed` when a seed carries it. `retired` and `retireAge` stay (V7, the household and the budget read them) and are kept consistent: `stoppedWork ≤ now` ⇒ `retired: true`; ahead ⇒ `retired: false`, `retireAge` = the age reached in that month (`ageOnDate`), `startMonth` = that month rather than the birthday guess of `retirementMonth` (`PlanTiming.js:126-136`), which stays the fallback. It changes no figure by itself (the saved `firstTaxYear` wins), so it is always writable, lock or no lock — a named entry in `LOCKED_BOOKKEEPING.stress` (`src/services/LockedPlanGuard.js:76-78`). It is **not** derived or pinned: a plan that never says when work stopped behaves exactly as today.
- **`firstTaxYear`** — meaning unchanged (year 0), but any tax year. Default in retired mode: `taxYearStartOf(stoppedWork)` when set; otherwise exactly today's rule (`thisTY + 1` in `migrateTiming`/`deriveTiming`, this tax year from the wizard's "pick up from here").
- **Derived by `deriveTiming`, never saved:** `planYearNow` (signed, `thisTY − firstTaxYear`), `elapsedYears = max(0, planYearNow)`, `monthsIntoYear` (months since 6 April of the current plan year, 0 before the start), `stoppedMonth`. `describeTiming` (`PlanTiming.js:328-347`) says "Plan started 6 April {year} (tax year {year}/{yy}); today is plan year {N} of {length}" when `planYearNow ≥ 0`.
- **On the plan object** (`planFromSettings`, `stressTest.js:187-228`): `yearOffset: elapsedYears`, `monthsIntoYear`, `runFirstTaxYear: firstTaxYear + elapsedYears`; `firstTaxYear` stays the plan's year 0; `trimPlan` keeps the three.
- **On the document:** nothing new to write — `timing` is spread in whole (`PlanDocument.js:208`), so `doc.timing.elapsedYears` is there for every document locked after the change and absent (read as 0) on every document before it. `pots.asOfPlanYear = elapsedYears` beside `pots.sipp` (`:198`) says which plan year the pot entered belongs to.
- **Scenario root, later releases (I, J):** `closedAt`, `nextPlanId`, `previousPlanId`, `before`, `flexibleAccess`.

### How `deriveTiming` reads them

Retired mode (`PlanTiming.js:236-243`): `firstTaxYear` = saved year, else `taxYearStartOf(stoppedWork)`, else `thisTY + 1`; then `planYearNow`, `elapsedYears`, `monthsIntoYear` as above; `yearsToStart` and `bridgeMonths` stay floored at 0, so nothing moves for a future start. Future mode (`:225-234`): unchanged, except `startMonth` from `stoppedWork` when present. `migrateTiming` (`:161-162`): for `retired: true` with `stoppedWork` the derived year is the stop's tax year and the "never before this tax year" floor does not apply to it; without `stoppedWork`, byte-identical. `staleDraft` (`:365-377`): returns null when `retired === true`, `stoppedWork` is set and the saved year is at or after the stop's tax year — the plan is running, not stale; the offer stays for `future` and `legacy` drafts and for a retiree with no stop month. `startYearChoices` (`:389-395`): in retired mode, the stop's tax year and every year from it to next April, labelled by tax year ("2025/26 — the year you stopped work"); "(saved)" only for a year outside that range.

### The default for every existing plan

`stoppedWork` is absent on every plan today, so `deriveTiming` derives exactly what it derives now, and `elapsedYears` is 0 for every plan whose saved year is this tax year or later — all of them, bar a year saved by hand in the past (none is known; the V7 test corpus makes one). With `elapsedYears = 0` the transformation below is the identity (`slice(0)`, `− 0`, pro-rate by 12/12), so every run, cone, document, plan of record and household figure is byte-identical; the replay tests under `tests/replay/` prove it. A plan with a hand-saved past year changes — it was wrong before.

## 4. Migration of existing plans

**Drafts and plans without a stop month.** Nothing moves on load. A retired plan with no `stoppedWork` shows one question, once, in the Timing block and on the V7 About step: "When did you stop work?" Answering writes `stoppedWork`; if its tax year is earlier than the saved start, the block offers "Start the plan from that tax year" — accepted only through the ordinary Save on a draft (the end age kept, as `index.html:7742-7744` does; step 1 re-anchored to the age reached that year, `:7769`) or through the action below on a locked plan. "Leave it" is remembered (`staleDraftDismissedFor` already exists, `PlanTiming.js:375`).

**A locked plan with records whose start moves back (e.g. a start moved back two years).** This cannot be a schema migration step (`migrations.js:13-19`: no step may touch a locked plan's Decision settings, Stress settings, history, document or plan of record; the engines reader confirmed each attempt abandons the chain), nor a load-time re-derivation (`StressRepository.js:115-118` writes nothing when a year is stored), nor a quiet Stress-side write (the value-blind `TIMING_PIN_KEYS` allowance at `LockedPlanGuard.js:76-78` lets `firstTaxYear` move on the Stress copy while the Decision copy — the one `decisionAnchorYear` prefers, `PlanTiming.js:319` — is refused; two tools would disagree). It is a deliberate, confirmed action, **"Move the plan's start"**, offered on a locked retired plan when the stop's tax year is earlier than the saved start. It shows exactly what changes (like the unlock modal, `index.html:11689-11708`) and then, in order:

1. **Archive** the plan document (`archivePlanDocument`, `ScenarioRepository.js:634`) and the plan of record (`archivePlanOfRecord`, `:605`) — as unlock does (`PlanLock.unlockPlan`, `:74-79`) — **without lifting the lock** and without `unlockPatchesOf` (`PlanCommit.js:150-153`): a plan locked before 6.19.0/6.22.0 must not acquire charges of 0.5% and a "Mostly cash" ISA because its start moved.
2. **One scenario write** under a new named allowance, `reAnchor`, in `LockedPlanGuard`: `stressTool.settings { stoppedWork, firstTaxYear: {stop tax year}, shapeAgeNow: the age reached in it, duration + the years moved }` and `decisionTool.settings { firstTaxYear: {stop tax year} }` — both copies to the same value, nothing else; and the pin allowance narrowed to "only when the stored key is absent", which is what the pin does.
3. **The checksum moves** (`firstTaxYear` is hashed). The records were made under this plan; only its year count changed. So the same action **re-stamps `settingsChecksum`** on every record to the new value (history is writable on a locked plan, `saveActiveHistory`, `ScenarioRepository.js:849`). `unlockCount` stays 0, so `PlanLock.js:39-40` would not dim them anyway, and the next real unlock will not dim them for the wrong reason.
4. **Rewrite on each record only the derived fields that name the old anchor** — `yearNum` recomputed, `bridgeYear` and `planStartYear` removed, `strategySource`/`strategyNote` replaced when they are the run-up words (`models/Decision.js:126-135`). Everything else byte for byte: the dates, the figures, the tax years. The tax-year set-ups hold no plan-year field; the wizard reads the anchor live (`TaxYearWizardService.js:300-303`), so 2026/27 becomes plan year 1 and its schedule path applies.
5. **Rebuild the plan of record** from the new Decision settings (`planOfRecordFor`, rows from year `elapsed`, lived rows padded) and **rebuild the plan document** (`buildPlanDocumentNow`, `index.html:7249-7262`; the archive step at `:7297-7300` already exists): the strategy re-run as seen from today — the ladder priced for 2026/27 onward with cash from now, no lived year bought, `bridgeCash` 0, `pots.asOfPlanYear: 1`. The old document and plan of record are in the archives, dated.
6. **A journey entry**: "Plan start moved to {stop tax year} (stopped work {month year}); the document and plan of record were rewritten" (`saveActiveJourney`, `:652`; the document keeps the journey, `PlanDocument.js:209`).

**The History page after:** the recorded months read their new plan years and, once back-filled, the lived years from "Year 0"; no ◌ and no "recorded under previous settings"; plan-vs-actual lists the current year against the new plan of record and the earlier years' draws against the schedule; the "months recorded before the plan starts" note is gone; the strip says "Plan year {N} of {length} — tax year 2026/27, age {age}"; the ladder overlay (`index.html:8006-8020`) keys its cash-paid branch on `ladderPosition().phase === 'before'` ("your cash pays until the first rung, {first rung year}"), not the plan year. The figures move — the State Pension's plan year, the end age, the ladder's cost without the lived years and with one more cash year counted from today — and the release note says so.

**As built (4 Nov 2026, the release after 6.52.0).** The action is `services/PlanMove.js` (the rules) and `answers/plan/moveStart.js` (the one write), sent by the planner's Timing block; the guard's `reAnchor` allowance is `PlanMove.reAnchorAllowance`. Three things differ from steps 1–6 above, each because the list in step 2 would have moved figures the design says must not move. (i) The write carries more keys than `stoppedWork`, both `firstTaxYear`s, `shapeAgeNow` and `duration`: the income by year (`targetSchedule`) wins over the steps in every engine and counts from year 0, so without moving it and the first step (`incomeSteps`) every later step would have come as many years earlier in age as the start moved (against decision 4); the same holds for the figures dated by plan year (a final-salary pension, the State Pension by year where no date is given, income streams, one-off spends, lump sums), which now keep their tax years; and the Decision copy, which carries the same keys and is the one the Decision tool and the plan of record read, moves with the Stress copy. The guard lets each through only at exactly the value the move works out from the plan as stored. (ii) The Decision copy also takes `lockedPosition`: where the lock's pots stood under the new year 0, read on the day the plan was locked (its pots are that day's), so the plan reads right even before the new document is written. The document and the plan of record are then run from that position (for fixture 03 with a stop in 2023/24, plan year 3, three months in), not from today. (iii) The pin allowance is narrowed both in the guard and in `timingPinPatch`, which now writes only absent keys. One write carries the settings, the records, both archives, the new document, the new plan of record and the journey, so a failure leaves the plan as it was. The design's `copied: true` is 6.50.0's `filledIn: true` (with its date); the fill now reaches back to the plan's first month (`MonthFill.planFillStart`), and a tax year not set up is set up from there first, as before. V7 asks the month on a locked plan's "About you and when" and opens the planner's action by the hand-over; it does not send the move itself (the parity ledger's `timing.move-start`). Its review (5 Nov 2026) widened "every figure counted from year 0": the counts of years too (a gilt ladder's and Bridge & engine's cash years, Ladder & ratchet's ladder years, a phased start), in both copies, every way's stash and the strategy block, so the ladder's first gilt keeps its tax year; a first step that falls or glides keeps its slope where it was, with a level step at its starting figure in front, and an old "Declining with age" profile is fixed at the old start first; the State Pension's default year field (12, read as "at 67") is left to follow the start age, and a hand-given year that would land on 12 is not offered the move. The Decision tool's pot floors and the plan of record's start from the pots as entered, in the plan year they belong to (`legacyDecision` `potsYear`; §3's "potsAsAtYear"). The month work stopped stays changeable on a locked plan, so a mistyped month is not kept for good.

**Back-fill** (the second ask) is a separate, small feature on the same page: "Record these figures for every month from {first} to {last}" — the batch today only runs forward from the entered month and stops at a tax-year boundary (`index.html:5760-5770`), each month must be free (`:5686-5690`), and the earlier tax year must be set up first. Records written this way are marked `copied: true`.

## 8. Read against how people actually start (3 Nov 2026)

We asked for research on how someone starting to use a tool like this might want to start: there are probably
tens of situations. Five strands were read (the official UK guidance journeys, about fifty first posts on the UK
forums, the onboarding of thirty UK and overseas tools, the national data on retirement pathways, and the life events
that restart a plan); the report and its notes are kept outside the repository with the session's research. What it
changes here:

- **A single retirement date is the wrong building block for everyone.** People keep at least
  four dates apart: when they stopped (per person, and per job for someone who cut down first), when the plan starts,
  when they first drew taxable money, and when each income begins. Of non-retired people over 55, 35% have already taken
  pension money (FCA Financial Lives 2024); the median first access is 60, the average exit from work 65. Every UK tool
  checked counts from today with one "retirement age"; three refuse anyone who has already drawn. Sections 1–3 keep the
  stop and the start apart and accept both in the past; that stands.
- **The first taxable draw is the person's fact from the first day, not only on a close (J).** It sets the Money
  Purchase Annual Allowance (£10,000 a year for life, employer's contributions included) and it is often before the
  plan existed. `flexibleAccess: { since }` is asked when the plan is made ("Have you taken taxable money from a pension
  yet? When?") as well as carried on every close; the Accumulation planner reads it beside the history; a saving spell
  after it shows the cap. Case B (drawing while working) and J depend on it; C should record it.
- **Tax-free cash already taken is the other fact that never resets.** It counts against the £268,275 lump sum
  allowance whichever plan is open. The preview's answers already ask it (`you.taxFreeTaken`, a yes/no); the plan needs
  the amount, carried on a close and read by any strategy that takes a tax-free part.
- **Close and succeed is the general mechanism, not a special case for one switch.** The same close (`closedAt`,
  `nextPlanId`, the carried facts) serves a switch to an annuity or a gilt ladder, a partner's death (the survivor's plan
  starts; the partner's age at death carried for the inherited pot), a divorce (a share carried out within four months),
  moving abroad and going into care. Section 2 I is the first user of it; the others are named here so its data is not
  shaped to one.
- **Ask what happened before asking for an age.** The guidance and the forums open with the situation, not the
  retirement age: still working; cutting down and drawing; stopped but not drawing yet; already drawing; retired for
  years and never touched the pension; someone else's pot; then events by name (the State Pension starting, redundancy or
  ill health, back to work, a partner's death, divorce, an annuity or ladder, a windfall or big spend, moving abroad).
  The planner's setup wizard and the preview's front door should start there; the dates follow from the answer. A
  release of its own, after 6.50.0.
- **Checks the tool must not make:** refusing a stop date in the past, a plan start later than the stop, a plan start
  earlier than today, pay-ins after the first draw, or a plan that ends because another takes over.

Situations the research adds that need nothing new in the model, only words or a dated line already supported: stopping
