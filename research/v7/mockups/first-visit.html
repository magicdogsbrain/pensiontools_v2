Behind it: the stage strip is the front door's life line reduced to three segments with the dot on the current one. The to-do list is at most three items, the first pink; "Soon" items are amber and not counted. The year view is the record keeper's picture: twelve cells, a purple bar for what was drawn, a dashed line for what the plan said, dashed cells for months filled in later, the pink ring on the month due. Everything below "Tools" is collapsed by default.

What makes it pop, and what keeps it honest

Pink is reserved for the one thing to press on each screen. The three stage colours never mean anything else. The status colours (green, amber, red) appear only on a verdict's edge. Every picture is drawn from the person's own numbers, to scale, with the bad case shaded, so the drama is real. Motion is limited to the slider redrawing the line and a door lifting on hover.
